# debris: what the game does

*Every piece left behind has a story.*

A phone game for kids aged 7 to 13. They pick up real litter, scan it with the phone, and every piece they put in a bin unlocks the true story of a machine people left on the Moon, on Mars or out in space. Play it at https://spaceapps-production.up.railway.app. Nothing to install, no account.

## Playing

- **Scan litter.** The camera opens in the page. An AI looks at the photo and says what the litter is and which bin it goes in: white for recycling, green for food scraps, black for everything else, or a special drop-off that a grown-up handles.
- **Hear a story.** Each piece unlocks the next of 13 chapters from the team's story, *Abandoned but Not Forgotten*. The chapters run from Surveyor 3, the robot that tested the Moon's ground, to Ingenuity, the first helicopter on another planet. Each one covers:
  - where the machine is now, what it was and what it did;
  - why it was left there and how it still helps us;
  - a "Did you know?" fact, with links to its sources (mostly NASA).

  A **Listen** button reads it aloud.
- **Bin it, earn a patch.** Tapping "It's in the bin!" earns that chapter's patch. With no bin nearby, the child can save the piece for later.
- **Explorer jacket.** All 13 patches go on a jacket the child can spin in 3D. Tap a patch to hear its story again.
- **Finale.** After the 13th patch comes a celebration with every patch and a map of where the 13 machines rest today. Kids can keep scanning after that, and nothing is ever lost.

## Two storytellers

- **Space Explorer**, with Eagle the lander as the guide. This is the starting choice.
- **The Bull**, inspired by the traditional bull dance of Florianópolis. He dances in to a short verse and a festive tune.
  - After each piece goes in the bin, the Dragon gobbles it up ("Chomp!"). The Bull then wakes a sleeping space explorer and tells its story.
  - The facts are the same as in Space Explorer.
- Kids can switch storyteller at any time without losing anything.

## Safe by design

- **Four safety rules come first:** gloves on, bring a grown-up, never touch anything sharp or broken, and photos aren't kept.
- **Sharp or dangerous finds** (broken glass, needles, chemicals) get a "Stop! Don't touch it." screen that says to ask an adult. They never count for a patch, so a child never has a reason to touch them.
- **Photos that don't count:** pictures of people, faces or screens, and things that aren't litter. Eagle asks for another photo. If the AI gets something wrong, "That's not right, scan again" drops it.

## Private by design

- **No account, email, payments or ads.** Kids pick a made-up space nickname and a crew buddy, and both stay on the phone.
- **Photos are checked once and then deleted.** They aren't kept on the server, in logs or on the phone.
- **The server keeps only counts.** Each phone can send a limited number of photos a day.

## Everyone's impact

- **The Impact screen** shows how many pieces of litter all players have picked up.
  - The total grows into a "litter tower" that stacks every piece 10 cm high. It is compared with famous tall things on the way to the Moon.
  - Only pieces actually binned count: never a scan alone or a dangerous find.
- **The pilot** is in Florianópolis.

## Made for every kid

- **Languages:** English, Spanish and Portuguese. The Bull speaks English and Portuguese.
- **Any phone browser:** it works on small screens, with big buttons.
- **Accessibility:** it passes automated accessibility checks (WCAG 2.1 AA rules) for screen readers and contrast, and it calms its animations for kids who turn motion down.

## For grown-ups, teachers and judges

- **A grown-ups section** covers the safety rules, how photos are handled, where the stories come from (links to the NASA and science pages behind each chapter) and live usage numbers.
- **Explore all the stories** opens any chapter without scanning, and patches stay the same.
- **Demo mode:** add `?demo` to the address to try every screen without a camera or the AI. Eagle pretends to scan, and demo play is kept apart from real play.
- **Start over** clears the nickname, patches and cleanup log from the phone.
